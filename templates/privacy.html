Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or government-issued identification.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Spendly service — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and enabling filtering by date or category. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing the app's core functionality.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical precautions — including encrypted storage and access controls — to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or database services) to operate the app; these providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Deleting your account will permanently remove all associated expense records and personal information from our systems. To exercise these rights, use the account settings within the app.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided within the app. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
